The wildcard
FL

Florida has the largest ACA market in the country — and the least visible data. State "trade secret" rules redact carrier-level enrollment, so the 15.3% average below is unweighted, not a true statewide figure.

Carriers are asking for anywhere from 3.9% to 39%. Because Florida is so large, wherever its real weighted number lands could shift the national average by a couple of points on its own.

What this means depending on your coverage

If you're subsidized
Your tax credit generally rises with the benchmark plan, so your out-of-pocket cost may move less than the headline number — but it's worth re-checking at enrollment.
If you're unsubsidized
You're likely to feel the full increase directly. Comparing plans during open enrollment, rather than auto-renewing, is the main lever you have.
Either way
These are still preliminary filings in most states — the final number in your state could move before enrollment opens.
